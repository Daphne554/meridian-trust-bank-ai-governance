## 2. Organizational structure (relevant to governance)

- **Board Risk Committee** — oversight of enterprise risk, receives quarterly model risk reporting
- **Chief Risk Officer (CRO)** — Elena Marsh — owns enterprise risk framework, dotted line into AI governance
- **Model Risk Management (MRM) function** — reports to CRO, 4 FTE, validates models under SR 11-7; currently stretched thin covering both traditional statistical models and newer ML/AI systems
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** — owns third-party/vendor risk and technical security controls
- **Head of Data & Analytics** — owns the internal ML/AI systems build (this is the group whose models you'll be assessing)
- **Compliance function** — monitors fair lending, UDAAP, BSA/AML; historically hasn't had a dedicated AI/ML compliance specialist
- **No dedicated AI Governance Committee yet** — this is a known gap you can use as a Govern-function exercise (e.g., "design the charter for one")

**Three lines of defense:**
1. **First line:** Data & Analytics team and business units building/using models  currently does light self-testing before deployment
2. **Second line:** MRM + Compliance validates and monitors, but AI-specific validation methodology is immature
3. **Third line:** Internal Audit has flagged AI/ML governance as an emerging risk area in the last two audit cycles but hasn't done a deep-dive audit yet

## 3. AI/ML system inventory

### 3.1 Credit Decisioning Model ("CreditScore-ML")
- **Purpose:** Consumer credit approval/denial and pricing for unsecured personal loans and credit cards
- **Type:** Gradient-boosted tree ensemble (XGBoost), replaced a logistic regression scorecard in 2023
- **Data inputs:** Traditional bureau data, income, existing account behavior, application data
- **Risk profile:** High - directly subject to ECOA/Reg B, FCRA adverse action requirements, fair lending disparate impact risk
- **Governance status:** Validated once at launch (2023), no revalidation scheduled, adverse action reason codes generated by a secondary rules layer that doesn't always map cleanly to the ML model's actual feature importance

### 3.2 Fraud Detection System ("SentryAI")
- **Purpose:** Real-time transaction fraud scoring for debit/credit card transactions
- **Type:** Ensemble of gradient boosting + rules engine, vendor-licensed core model (third-party) with in-house tuning layer
- **Risk profile:** Medium-high false positives cause customer friction and complaints (UDAAP-adjacent), false negatives are direct financial loss; third-party model risk applies (OCC vendor risk guidance)
- **Governance status:** Vendor provides a model card but Meridian has never independently validated the vendor's training data or bias testing; contract is up for renewal this year (good scenario for third-party risk exercises)

### 3.3 AML Transaction Monitoring Alerts ("Watchtower")
- **Purpose:** Generates suspicious activity alerts for BSA/AML compliance team review
- **Type:** Anomaly detection model (unsupervised) layered over legacy rules-based system
- **Risk profile:** High regulatory consequence if it under-alerts (BSA/AML enforcement risk) but massive alert fatigue if it over-alerts (currently a known operational pain point 92% of alerts are closed as false positives)
- **Governance status:** No formal drift monitoring; model has degraded since a 2024 tuning pass but no one owns re-tuning cadence

### 3.4 Customer Service Chatbot ("Meridian Assistant")
- **Purpose:** Public-facing LLM-based chatbot for account questions, embedded in mobile app and website
- **Type:** Fine-tuned third-party LLM via API, with a retrieval layer over internal policy documents
- **Risk profile:** Medium - reputational/consumer harm risk if it gives incorrect account or product information, potential UDAAP exposure if it makes claims that mislead consumers; also a data leakage risk if not properly scoped
- **Governance status:** Launched by a product team without MRM involvement 

### 3.5 Internal Underwriting Copilot ("UW Copilot")
- **Purpose:** Internal tool that drafts summary recommendations for commercial loan underwriters using an LLM over loan file documents
- **Type:** Third-party LLM (enterprise API), retrieval-augmented over internal document repository
- **Risk profile:** Medium — human-in-the-loop (underwriters review and can override), but risk of automation bias (underwriters rubber-stamping AI drafts) and confidential business data handling
- **Governance status:** Has an AI use policy acknowledgment click-through for underwriters but no monitoring of override rates or usage patterns

### 3.6 Marketing Personalization Engine ("Engage")
- **Purpose:** Personalizes marketing offers and product recommendations to existing customers
- **Type:** Collaborative filtering + propensity models
- **Risk profile:** Lower-medium — but overlaps with fair lending if product offer eligibility correlates with protected class proxies (e.g., geography-based redlining risk)
- **Governance status:** Owned entirely by Marketing, no compliance sign-off process currently required before campaign launch
